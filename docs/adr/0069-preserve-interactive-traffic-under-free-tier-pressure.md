# Preserve interactive traffic under capacity or cost pressure

When the deployment approaches an included Cloudflare allowance or configured cost threshold, it preserves attendance registration pages, Organization administration, and authentication callbacks before background automation.

Inbox processing, AI extraction, ordinary LINE notification, and their retries may be paused before interactive traffic. Paused work remains explicitly pending in the owning Organization D1 database, produces an Operations warning, and resumes oldest-first after the relevant allowance recovers. The system must not discard work or report delivery success merely because included Cloudflare capacity is unavailable.
